## Literfy

One-liner:

Literfy is an AI academic research tool that helps students and researchers find papers, organize sources, and start literature reviews faster.

Target users:

- graduate students
- PhD students
- Master students
- researchers
- students writing literature reviews
- academic writers

Allowed features:

- Find Papers: search and discover academic papers based on a research topic.
- Paper Discovery / Related Papers: discover relevant or related papers for a research topic.
- Literature Review Outline: generate a structured outline for a literature review based on selected papers.
- AI Literature Review Draft: generate a structured literature review draft based on collected papers.
- Citation / Export Workflow: export citations or research materials into downstream writing workflows.

Source-grounding note:

Literfy generates literature review outlines and drafts based on real academic papers selected or discovered in the workflow.

Allowed claim boundaries:

- Can say Literfy works from real academic papers in the workflow.
- Can say Literfy helps generate a structured literature review draft based on selected/collected papers.
- Can say Literfy helps users move from real papers to an organized review starting point.
- Should still say users need to review, edit, and verify the output.

Pain scenarios:

- A student starts a literature review and opens 30-50 random tabs.
- A PhD or Master student does not know which papers are worth reading first.
- A researcher has papers but does not know how to organize them into a review structure.
- A student wastes hours before actually writing anything.

Forbidden claims:

- Do not say Literfy writes a perfect literature review.
- Do not say Literfy replaces real research.
- Do not say citations are always 100% correct.
- Do not tell users to submit AI output directly.
- Do not claim guaranteed acceptance by journals or professors.
- Do not claim the output is publication-ready without human review.

Good TikTok angles:

- If your literature review starts with 50 random tabs open, this is for you.
- This is how I start a literature review now.
- I stopped staring at a blank page before writing my literature review.
- Stop searching papers manually like this.
