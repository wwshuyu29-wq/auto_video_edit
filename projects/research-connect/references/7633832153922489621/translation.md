## Creative Structure

1. Authority hook: "PhD/Master student" creates status gap.
2. Familiar entry point: starts on Google Scholar to borrow academic trust.
3. Tool reveal: moves from Google Scholar to the creator's own assistant.
4. Simple steps: click researcher, type topic, select LaTeX/PDF.
5. Time promise: "2-3 mins".
6. Proof: shows a generated paper.
7. Bonus proof: visualizations, charts, structured document.

## Product Implication

This video is not really selling "Google Scholar." It uses Google Scholar as an academic trust object, then redirects attention to a different tool that promises a complete research output.

For our adaptation, the key is:

- Start with a familiar academic workflow.
- Name the user's painful output.
- Make the product look like the missing shortcut.
- End with visible proof, not abstract claims.
