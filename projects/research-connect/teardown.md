Status: TikTok page access was blocked during browsing, so this teardown is based on the user-provided screenshot.

## What To Imitate

The user wants to imitate the Google Scholar-style cover and subtitle hook system, especially the circled posts in the screenshot.

This should be treated as a format imitation, not direct copying:

- Familiar academic webpage background.
- Handheld screen recording/photo feel.
- Centered bold white captions with black outline.
- Hook written as a shortcut to a research-paper outcome.
- Repeated formula across many posts.

## Cover Formula

Visual base:

- A phone-shot or handheld shot of a laptop/tablet screen.
- Google Scholar homepage or an academic-search-like page.
- Mostly white page area, browser chrome visible at top.
- Slight tilt, glare, blur, moire, or imperfect framing.
- Dark gradient/vignette at the bottom, likely from TikTok grid preview or video overlay.

Caption placement:

- Center-middle or slightly lower than center.
- 3-5 lines.
- White bold text.
- Black outline/drop shadow.
- Very high contrast.
- No decorative design elements.

The visual works because the viewer instantly understands "academic research tool" before reading the hook.

## Underlying Creative Logic

The account repeats one core promise:

"You are using the common research workflow wrong. Here is a smarter academic shortcut."

The format combines:

- Authority object: Google Scholar / academic interface.
- Audience identity: student, PhD/Master student, RRL/literature review writer.
- Gap: normal student vs PhD/Master/Harvard/smart way.
- Friction: outdated website, ChatGPT failure, Google Scholar being hard.
- Shortcut: easy way, smart way, legit way.

## Why It Works

Audience fit:

- Students and early researchers already know Google Scholar.
- They have anxiety around papers, RRL, citations, and literature review.
- They want legitimacy, not just AI writing.

Scroll-stopping reason:

- The cover looks like a real workflow, not a polished ad.
- The hook promises a method upgrade.
- "PhD/Master", "Harvard", "legit", and "not GPT" create authority and curiosity.

Production advantage:

- Very cheap to make.
- Same background can support many scripts.
- The account trains viewers to recognize the format.

## Transferable Template

```text
[Still using / How to / I found] + [common academic task/tool]
+ [status gap or pain]
+ ([easy/smart/legit/Harvard/PhD way])
```

Good variants:

- "Still using Google Scholar like this?"
- "How to find better papers for your RRL (the smart way)"
- "How to write a literature review without drowning in tabs"
- "How PhD students find sources faster (not the obvious way)"
- "Stop asking ChatGPT to write your RRL. Do this first."
- "How to turn 20 papers into a clean review map"

## Adaptation Rules For Our Product

Use this format only if the product genuinely helps with academic research, paper search, paper reading, RRL, literature review, citation, summarization, or research writing.

Map each hook to a real product feature:

- "find better papers" -> search/import/discovery feature.
- "write RRL" -> paper extraction, themes, synthesis, outline, citation support.
- "not GPT" -> evidence-linked answers, source-grounded writing, paper-based reasoning.
- "PhD/Master way" -> workflow, methodology, structured reading, comparison matrix.

Avoid unsupported claims:

- Do not claim the product writes publication-ready papers unless true.
- Do not imply official Google Scholar affiliation.
- Do not use "Harvard way" unless the method is clearly a style/quality comparison, not institutional endorsement.

## Trademark / Platform Risk

Showing Google Scholar as a familiar website can work as screen content, but building the product's whole ad identity around the Google Scholar logo may create brand/trademark risk.

Safer adaptation:

- Use a Google Scholar-like research search page for the first frame if it is part of the workflow.
- Transition quickly to the user's own product.
- Use text like "Google Scholar workflow" rather than implying partnership.
- Consider an "academic search page" mockup for repeated cover templates.
